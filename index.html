Built on discipline, not shortcuts.

NABZAP LLC is a Georgia limited liability company founded in 2026 and based in Acworth, Georgia. We buy genuine, new products and sell them to customers online, on the marketplaces where they already shop.

Our founder, Hamed Jalalzada, spent more than 20 years in finance, compliance and global operations for an international humanitarian organization, managing large multi-country budgets, partner agreements, procurement and audits. NabZap is run the same way: documented sourcing, clean records and commitments that are kept.

We are a young company and we say so plainly. What we offer partners is care with their products, straight communication and a business built to grow steadily.

Mission

NabZap sources quality products from trusted brands and suppliers and brings them to customers through accurate, reliable, multi-channel online retail.

Vision

To grow NabZap into a trusted, scalable commerce company that brands and distributors choose as a partner and customers return to.

For brands and distributors: a retailer that protects your brand.

We are opening wholesale accounts with brands and authorized distributors in toys, collectibles and seasonal gifts. Here is what you can expect from us.

Our commitments

  • Your pricing policy, followed. We honor minimum advertised price (MAP) and any channel restrictions you set.
  • Accurate listings. We sell on your existing product pages and never create duplicate or altered listings.
  • Genuine, new product only. Everything we sell is new, in original packaging, and bought through channels you approve.
  • Careful fulfilment. Orders are packed to arrive in the condition your customers expect, and customer questions are answered promptly.
  • Prompt payment and clear records. Purchase orders, invoices and payments are documented and on time.
