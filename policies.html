Last updated October 7, 2026

Orders, shipping and returns

NabZap does not take orders or payments on this website. Our products are sold through our storefronts on Amazon, Walmart Marketplace and eBay.

When you buy from us on one of those marketplaces, your order is covered by that marketplace's shipping, return and buyer-protection policies, and you can track, return or ask about the order from your account there. If you need help with an order, you can also write to support@nabzap.com with your order number.

Everything we sell is new and in its original packaging unless the listing says otherwise.

Privacy

This website does not have accounts, a checkout or contact forms, and we do not use it to collect personal information.

If you email us, we use your name, email address and the contents of your message only to reply and to keep a record of our correspondence. We do not sell or share that information.

Our website host may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service.

For orders placed on a marketplace, the marketplace shares with us the details needed to fulfil the order, such as your name and delivery address. We use them only for that purpose.

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to support@nabzap.com.
