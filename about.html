Built on discipline, not shortcuts.

NABZAP LLC is a Georgia limited liability company founded in 2026 and based in Acworth, Georgia. We buy genuine, new products and sell them to customers online, on the marketplaces where they already shop. Our range today is toys, collectibles and seasonal gifts, and we are expanding into tools and home improvement, and home and kitchen.

NabZap was founded by Hamed Jalalzada, an operations leader with more than 22 years in international humanitarian work. He began as a field accountant and rose to direct global business operations, leading a team of grants managers and a funding portfolio that grew tenfold on his watch.

Across programs in Asia, Africa and Latin America he was accountable for more than $900 million in funding from U.S. government, corporate, private and individual donors, including multi-million-dollar endowment funds, with an active portfolio that reached nearly $300 million. He managed multi-year awards across their full life cycle, from pre-award through partner agreements, procurement, reporting and audits to close-out.

He is also a hands-on DIY builder who knows tools from using them. NabZap is run with the same discipline: documented sourcing, clean records and commitments that are kept.

We are a young company and we say so plainly. What we offer partners is care with their products, straight communication and a business built to grow steadily.

Mission

NabZap sources quality products from trusted brands and suppliers and brings them to customers through accurate, reliable, multi-channel online retail.

Vision

To grow NabZap into a trusted, scalable commerce company that brands and distributors choose as a partner and customers return to.
