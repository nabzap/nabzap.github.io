Questions brands and distributors ask us

Where does NabZap sell?

We sell to customers in the United States through our storefronts on Amazon, Walmart Marketplace and eBay.

Do you follow minimum advertised price (MAP) policies?

Yes. We honor your MAP policy and any channel restrictions you set, on every marketplace we sell on.

How do you list our products?

We add our offer to your existing product pages, using your titles, images and identifiers. We do not create duplicate or altered listings.

What documents can you provide?

A Georgia resale certificate, our federal EIN and Form W-9, our Georgia business registration, and links to our marketplace storefronts. Ask and we will send them.

How are orders fulfilled?

Through Amazon's and Walmart's fulfilment programs, and directly from our own stock in Georgia. Everything ships new, in original packaging.

What size of orders do you place?

We open with a test order sized to your minimums, then reorder based on how the product sells. Tell us your minimum order and case-pack sizes and we will work to them.

How long has NabZap been operating?

NABZAP LLC was founded in Georgia in 2026. We are a young company, led by a founder with more than 22 years of experience in finance, compliance and operations.

How do we open an account with you?

Email support@nabzap.com with your reseller application or price list, or call (770) 913-6650. We reply within one business day.
